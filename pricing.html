04 — Pricing questions

The money questions

All questions
Why does the price change with the vehicle?
Because the work does. A 7-seater has more carpet, more seats and more glass than a coupe — pricing them the same would mean overcharging the coupe. The surcharge is Coupes & Sedans +$0, SUVs & Crossovers +$15, Trucks & 7-Seaters +$35.
Is the price on this page the price I pay?
For a package on a normal car, yes — the tier price plus your vehicle class, and nothing added at the end. Add-ons with a range are confirmed with you before we start, once we have seen the vehicle.
What does the deposit do?
$50–$100 holds the appointment and comes off your total, so it is not an extra cost. Reschedule at least 24 hours ahead and it carries to the new date. Applied to your total. Refundable with 24 hours’ notice.
Do you charge to drive out to me?
No. No travel fee inside Winnipeg city limits. If you are outside city limits, reach out and we will see what we can do.
