The problem

Salt, gravel, and six months of it.

A Winnipeg winter puts salt in your carpets, sand in your seat rails, and a film on your paint that a drive-through wash will not touch. Spring adds pollen and the first hatch of bugs. By June most cars in this city are carrying eight months of road on them.

We deal with the parts you can see and the parts you can only smell. Not a rinse — extraction, decontamination, and treatment, done in the order that actually works.

07 — Before you book

The questions
we get asked most.

All questions
Do I need to be home during the detail?
Not necessarily. As long as we have access to the vehicle and a water/power source nearby, you’re free to go about your day. Most clients leave the keys and let us handle it.
How long does a typical detail take?
The Minimal package takes about 1-1.5 hours. The Standard runs 2-3 hours. Premium and add-on services like paint correction can take a full day depending on vehicle size and condition.
What if it rains on my appointment day?
We’ll reach out to reschedule at no extra charge. Interior details can still happen under covered areas like garages or carports. We’ll work with you to find the best option.
Do you bring your own water and power?
We come fully equipped with our own tools and supplies. We just need access to a standard outdoor outlet and water spigot for exterior work. Interior-only jobs don’t need either.
