Why Coat Your Paint

Protection you can see

Water beads off

A hydrophobic layer means rain, road salt, and dirt slide off instead of baking on. Winnipeg winters are hard on paint — this is the answer.

Deep, lasting gloss

The coating adds a wet, mirror-like shine that a wax can't hold. Your paint looks freshly detailed months longer.

Done at your door

No drop-off, no waiting room. We arrive fully equipped and coat your vehicle wherever it's parked across the city.

The Options

Spray or true ceramic

Not every car needs the same coating. Spray ceramic is the fast, affordable layer — a real boost in gloss and water resistance that holds for several months. It's the smart pick if you want protection without the full commitment.

True ceramic is the long-term play. It bonds to properly prepped paint and stands up for years, not months. Because prep and product tier matter, we quote it per vehicle after we see what you're working with.

Coating seals in whatever is underneath — so if your paint has swirls or light scratches, we'll correct it first. Pair it with paint correction and we handle both in one visit.
