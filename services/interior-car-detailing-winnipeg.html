The Packages

Three levels, one standard

Minimal is the reset — a thorough interior vacuum and general wipe-down to get things clean and tidy. It's the quick refresh between deeper details.

The Standard goes further with basic deep cleaning of fabrics and surface treatment for your interior materials. Premium is the full royal treatment: intensive fabric care, odor and stain management, and protective treatments that keep it looking done.

Pricing is fixed per vehicle class — SUVs and crossovers add $15, trucks and 7-seaters add $35. Add-ons like pet hair removal, stain extraction, and odor removal layer on top when you need them. Build your exact price on the booking form.
