What comes out

Salt, sand, dog hair, spilled coffee, and whatever is under the seat.

An interior gets dirty in layers. Loose debris sits on top. Under it, grit works into the carpet pile and the seat foam. Under that, liquid has already soaked through — coffee, pop, melted snow, whatever the kids were holding.

A vacuum only ever reaches the first layer. That is why a car can be vacuumed on Saturday and smell the same on Monday. We work down through all three.

01 — Covered on every tier

Every surface, every row.

Front, rear, trunk

Carpets and mats

Vacuumed, then extracted where the tier calls for it. Mats come out of the car — cleaning around them is how salt survives a detail.

Seats and upholstery

Cloth gets shampooed and extracted. Leather and vinyl get cleaned and conditioned so they do not dry and crack through a Winnipeg winter.

Hard surfaces

Dash, console, door cards, cupholders, vents, and the door jambs. The jambs are the part most places skip and the first thing you touch.

Glass, inside

Interior glass and mirrors, streak-free. The haze on the inside of a windshield is off-gassing from the dash — cleaning the dash is half the fix.

Trunk and cargo

Full vacuum and wipe-down including under the load floor. Spare-tire wells collect more than people expect.

Odor at the source

Included on Premium, available as an add-on to any tier. We treat what is causing the smell rather than covering it with scent.

03 — Interior questions

Asked and answered

All questions
What is the difference between a car wash vacuum and interior detailing?
A vacuum lifts what is loose on top. Detailing gets what has bonded to the fibre — salt that has dried into the carpet, grit worked into the seat foam, and the film on every hard surface. We use extraction, not suction alone.
Can you get salt stains out of carpets and mats?
Usually, yes. Dried road salt leaves a white bloom that re-appears after a surface clean because the salt is still in the fibre. Hot-water extraction pulls it out rather than redistributing it. Badly set-in salt on light carpet can leave a faint shadow — we will tell you before we start if we think that is the case.
Do you clean the trunk and the third row?
Yes. Trunk and every row of seating are part of the interior on all three packages. Vehicle class pricing exists because a 7-seater is genuinely more work than a Civic, not as a surcharge for the sake of one.
How long should I leave the car after an interior detail?
Give fabric a few hours with the windows cracked if the weather allows. Extraction leaves the material damp, not wet. In winter we will run it dry as far as we can before we finish.
