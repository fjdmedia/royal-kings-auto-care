The Work

Real correction, not a cover-up

Paint correction means machine-polishing the clear coat to level out the defects — swirls, wash marks, light scratches, and oxidation. Done right, the result is permanent, not a glaze that hides the damage until the next rain.

How many steps your paint needs depends on its condition, which is why we price it after we see it. Lighter jobs are a single stage; heavily marred paint takes multi-stage work. Either way you get an honest read before we start.

Deep scratches that have cut through the clear coat can be reduced but not always erased — we'll never promise a miracle. And once the paint is corrected, coating it locks the finish in.
